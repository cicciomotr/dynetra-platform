Physiology Legend

  • GSR: 0–5 μS Relaxed · 6–10 Activated · >10 High Stress
  • Breathing: 6–10 rpm Relaxed · 10–14 Activated · >14 High
  • Peripheral Temperature: >31°C Normal · <31°C Stress response
  • Heart Rate: <85 bpm Resting · >85 Physical activation
  • HRV RMSSD: >50 ms Good variability · <50 Reduced variability
  • Muscular Load: Low / Medium / High

● Green = Optimal | ● Yellow = Attention | ● Red = Critical | ● Blue = Informational

Physiological thresholds are generic reference values and should be calibrated against each driver's baseline.
